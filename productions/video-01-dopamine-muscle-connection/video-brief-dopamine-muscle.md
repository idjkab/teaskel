## 2. The Core Problem & Angle
- **The Viewer's Frustration:** You genuinely want to build an aesthetic, strong physique. You packed your gym bag. But at 5 PM, your brain feels like lead, you stare at your phone for 40 minutes, make up an excuse, and skip your workout. You feel guilty and believe you lack "discipline."
- **The Counter-Intuitive Angle:** Your muscles aren't tired; your central nervous system (CNS) and dopamine baseline are burnt out from 8 hours of micro-stimulation (infinite scrolling, multi-tabbing, processed sugar).
- **The Value Promise:** In this 5-minute video, we break down the neuro-muscular circuit and give you a bulletproof 3-step protocol you can execute today to restore maximum workout drive in under 24 hours.
