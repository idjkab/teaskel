# Teaskel Channel Playbook & Production Bible

**Channel Name:** Teaskel  
**Tagline:** Master Your Biology. Build Your Mind. Sculpt Your Physique.  
**Core Thesis:** Physical transformation and mental discipline are two sides of the same biological coin. We simplify neuroscience, hypertrophy mechanics, and stoic mindset into high-retention, actionable 2D animated masterclasses.

---

## 1. Character Identity & Visual Grammar

### Protagonist: Teaskel ("Tea")
- **Visual Design:** Sleek, modern 2D minimalist character with clean lines, expressive eyes/eyebrows, athletic proportion, and dynamic posture.
- **Color Palette:**
  - **Character Base:** Charcoal / Deep Slate (#1E293B) with crisp white outlines.
  - **Energy / High Dopamine / Drive:** Electric Amber & Gold (#F59E0B / #FBBF24).
  - **Calm / Focus / Baseline:** Deep Cyan & Neon Teal (#06B6D4 / #14B8A6).
  - **Stress / Friction / Distraction:** Crimson & Flame Red (#EF4444 / #F87171).
  - **Hypertrophy / Growth / Biomechanics:** Emerald Glow (#10B981).
- **Animation Rules:**
  - Minimum 1 visual transition / angle cut / zoom every 2.5 to 3.5 seconds.
  - Kinetic typography highlights key words as they are spoken.
  - Use expressive stickman gestures: slumped shoulders for dopamine crash, glowing aura for flow state, anatomical overlays for lifting cues.

---

## 2. Audio & Voiceover Direction

- **Voice Tone:** Authoritative, brotherly, calm yet intensely focused.
- **Pacing:** 145–155 words per minute.
- **Music Style:** Lo-fi atmospheric hip-hop blended with subtle cinematic stoic basslines and uplifting synth swells during breakthroughs.
- **Sound Effects (SFX):**
  - Text pops: Muffled wooden click or soft digital snap.
  - Scene wipes: Crisp low-pass whoosh.
  - Key Insights / "Aha!" moments: Resonant singing bowl chime or subtle sub-bass hit.
  - Lifting / Effort moments: Barbell plate clank, chalk clap.

---

## 3. High-CTR Packaging Formulas (Title & Thumbnail)

### Title Patterns:
1. **The Biological Reframe:** `Why You Are [Painful State] Even Though You [Effort Made] (The [Mechanism] Fix)`
   * Example: *Why You're Too Tired to Workout Even After 8 Hours of Sleep (The Dopamine Fix)*
2. **The Ancient Protocol:** `The [Historical Figure/Philosophy] [Time] Rule: How to [Desirable Outcome]`
   * Example: *The Spartan 3-Hour Rule: How Ancient Warriors Built Elite Bodies With Zero Motivation*
3. **The 1% Biological Leak:** `The "Silent Killer" of [Goal] (Stop Doing This)`
   * Example: *Why Your Physique Looks the Same After 6 Months (The 'Silent Killer' of Muscle Growth)*

### Thumbnail Formulas:
- **Left Side:** Relatable failure / Slumped character in dim red/blue light / Depleted energy battery.
- **Center:** High-contrast dividing line or transformation arrow.
- **Right Side:** Upright, glowing muscular protagonist / Full green battery / Clear neurochemical diagram.
- **Text on Thumbnail:** Maximum 2–4 bold words in high-contrast yellow or white (e.g., *"FIX YOUR BRAIN"*, *"STOP WASTING SETS"*, *"THE 24-HOUR RESET"*).
