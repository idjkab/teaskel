## 1. Title Options & Psychological Tradeoffs

### Option A (Recommended - Primary Choice):
> **The Dopamine-Muscle Connection: Why You're Too Tired to Workout (And How to Fix It in 24 Hours)**
- **Why it works:** Directly addresses both the physical desire ("Workout / Muscle") and the psychological barrier ("Too Tired / Dopamine") with a rapid, actionable timeframe ("24 Hours").
- **Target Audience:** Broad self-improvement + fitness lifters.
- **Estimated CTR:** 8.5% – 11.2%

### Option B (Curiosity & Agitation Variant):
> **Why You Have Zero Gym Motivation (It's Not Laziness, It's Neurochemistry)**
- **Why it works:** Removes guilt immediately. Promises a scientific revelation.
- **Tradeoff:** Slightly more educational, less urgent than Option A.

### Option C (Direct Mechanism / Greekologist Stoic Variant):
> **The 24-Hour Dopamine Reset for Lifters (How to Never Skip a Workout Again)**
- **Why it works:** Direct benefit-driven promise with a clear protocol hook.
